# Apex Control — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026_

Apex Control respects your privacy. In short: the game does not collect, store, or
transmit any personal data to us.

## Data we collect
None. Apex Control has no accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising. We never
see your gameplay.

## Microphone & voice control
Voice control is optional. When enabled, the microphone captures your spoken
instructions and converts them to text using **on-device speech recognition**
(`requiresOnDeviceRecognition`). Audio is processed live on your device, is not
recorded or stored, and is never transmitted to us or any third party. You can turn
voice control off in Settings and play entirely by touch. Microphone access can be
revoked at any time in the iOS Settings app.

## On-device data
Your scores, career statistics and settings are stored only on your device (in the
app's sandboxed Application Support directory and in `UserDefaults`). Deleting the
app removes them. You can also clear them at any time via **Settings ▸ Reset all
stats** inside the app.

## Game Center
If you choose to sign in, your scores and achievement progress are submitted to
Apple's Game Center. That information is handled by Apple under
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We only send your
score and achievement completion percentages; we do not receive or store your Game
Center identity on our own servers (we have none).

## Children
The game is suitable for all ages. It contains no data collection, no in-app
purchases, and no third-party content.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and mirrored inside
the app on the Privacy Policy screen.
